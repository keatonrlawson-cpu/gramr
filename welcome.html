Welcome to Gramr

Your grammar coach — it teaches you the rule instead of silently rewriting your words.

What the underlines mean

Red wavyErrors — misspellings, its/it's, could of, have went…
Amber dashedWarnings — who/whom, fewer/less, tense shifts, comma splices…
Blue dottedTips — wordy phrases, regional spelling, style suggestions…

How it works

  1. Click any underline to see what's wrong, why it matters, and a trick to remember the rule.
  2. Apply correction fixes it in one click (and earns XP). Ctrl+Z undoes it.
  3. Ignore — I meant this teaches Gramr your names and jargon; it won't flag them again.
  4. Open the toolbar popup for your level, streak, weekly focus rule, and a daily practice question. Pick your English variant (US/UK/AU/CA) there too.
  5. The tooltips shrink as you improve — once you've mastered a rule, Gramr stops lecturing you about it.

Try it right now

Click into the box, wait a beat, then click the underlines that appear. Every one of them is a lesson.

Gramr never sends your text anywhere — all checking happens on your device, and your progress is stored locally in your browser.
